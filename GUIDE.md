# Weekly guide for community managers

**Before Monday (Thu–Sun):** add next week's events, all at once or one by one.

*All at once: **Import***. Pick the week, then use whichever is easiest:
- ✍️ **Type or paste**: rough notes or a WhatsApp message.
- 📷 **Photo of notes**: a clear photo of the whiteboard or notebook.
- 🎙️ **Voice**: say each event in one go (name, day, time, place, price, RSVP).
- 📊 **Excel / CSV**: upload the template (download it from the Import page).
- 🟩 **Google Sheet**: keep the template in Google Sheets, share it as "Anyone with the link", paste the link.

Everything lands in a **check** table: red must be fixed, yellow is worth a look (like "morning or evening?"). Untick anything you don't want, then **Add events**.

*One by one:*
1. Open the studio → **Events** → **+ Add an event**.
2. Pick the location and tick the brands for the poster (NomadGao, The Hotpot House, or both).
3. Fill in the name, type, date, time, venue, entry fee and RSVP. Write one good line in **Short description**; it shapes the caption.
4. **Save and add another** for the next one. **Copy** on the events list repeats an event one week later.

**Monday:** make and share.
1. Open **Studio**. It shows the week you're planning (switch with ‹ ›).
2. Look at the joint calendar, your location's calendar and every event poster. Don't like one? **🎲 Shuffle** it. Want a whole new look? **Shuffle everything**.
3. Pick a size at the top (Story / Post / Square) to preview it.
4. Each card has three captions: **Instagram**, **WhatsApp groups**, **Local community**. Edit them freely, or press **✨ Rewrite with AI** for a fresh version.
5. Share:
   - **WhatsApp**: opens WhatsApp with the caption ready and the poster copied. Pick the group(s), press **Ctrl+V** to attach the poster, send. You can pick up to 5 chats at once.
   - **Share…**: your computer's share menu (image + caption together) when available.
   - **Download** / **All 3 sizes** for Instagram.
   - **Download all (ZIP)** at the top: every image in the sizes you ticked, plus a captions folder.

**Photos:** in **Photo gallery**, add 5–10 good photos per event type for your location. Posters pick a different one each shuffle. An event's own photo (added on its form) is always in the mix.

**RSVPs:** choose **Our RSVP form** on an event to get an RSVP page and QR code. When seats run out, people go on a waitlist. Admins see names and numbers in **Admin → RSVPs**.
